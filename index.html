Subclass 309 Grant Horizon

A probability estimate for when a partner visa is likely to be decided, fitted to the two processing benchmarks the Department of Home Affairs publishes for this subclass.

Subclass 309 Partner Visa Processing Time Estimator

Enter your lodgement date and current Department of Home Affairs 50% and 90% processing benchmarks. The tool uses a log-normal statistical model to produce a planning window.

Important: This is a statistical scenario model, not an official Department of Home Affairs decision predictor or a guarantee of an individual outcome.

How it works

The model fits the entered 50th and 90th percentile benchmarks exactly, then derives the 25th and 75th percentile dates from the same distribution. Seasonal and application-complexity controls shape the scenario for planning purposes.
